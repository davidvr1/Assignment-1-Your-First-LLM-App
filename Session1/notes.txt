Company Travel Policy

All employees are eligible for reimbursement of business travel expenses,
including flights, trains, and hotel stays, provided the trip is approved
in advance by a manager. Reimbursement requests must be submitted within
30 days of the trip's end date, along with itemized receipts.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Alcohol is
not covered under any circumstances. Employees traveling internationally
are eligible for an additional $25 per day allowance to cover incidental
expenses such as visas or local transit.

Employees are expected to book the most economical travel option
available. Business class flights are only permitted for flights longer
than 8 hours in duration, and only with prior written approval from a
department head.

Personal vehicle use for business purposes is reimbursed at a rate of
$0.60 per mile. Parking and toll fees are also reimbursable with receipts.

This policy does not cover relocation expenses, which are governed by a
separate Relocation Assistance policy available from HR.
